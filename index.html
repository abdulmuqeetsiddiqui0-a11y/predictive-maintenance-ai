How the model works

Eight signals are scaled from 0 to 1: vibration level, vibration trend, temperature level, temperature trend, service overdue ratio, failures in the past year, machine age and upcoming production load. A weighted logistic model turns them into a probability of failure within 14 days. Vibration is projected forward to the alarm limit (7 mm/s) to estimate remaining useful life. Rules read the combination of signals to name the failure pattern, and the planner picks the lowest-load day before the predicted failure for maintenance. Weights are expert-set starting values; with real failure logs they can be fitted with logistic regression, survival analysis or gradient boosting.
